Check your eligibility for a grant

This form will check if you are eligible for a grant based on the following criteria:

  • Your address must not have previously been given a grant.
  • Your car must be an approved make and model.
  • Your property must be in England, Wales, Scotland, or Northern Ireland (not in the Channel Islands or the Isle of Man).

Later, you will also need to submit evidence that the location of the charger is a legal public parking space and does not obstruct the street.

If you are eligible, you will be able to begin an application directly from this form.
