Electric Vehicle Charger Grant Service

You can use this service to:

  • Check your eligibility for a grant for installing an electric vehicle (EV) charger outside your home.
  • If you are eligible, begin an application.
  • Check the status of and update any existing applications.
  • Check which installers are approved for receiving the grant.

Start now

Before you start

To check your eligibility and apply, you will need to know:

  • The address of the property you would like to install the charger at.
  • The registration number of the electric vehicle
  • The make and model of the electric vehicle.

You will also need to provide proof (including photographs) that there is legal on-street parking near your home that does not obstruct other vehicles or pedestrians.

We will collect your email address so that we can contact you when your application is updated and when action is needed.

After the installation

Once you have had the charger installed you will need to tell us:

  • The approved installation company who installed your charger.
  • The cost of the installation.
  • The date when the installation took place.
  • Your bank details, so that the grant can be paid to you.

The installer will need to confirm the details of the installation before the grant can be paid.
